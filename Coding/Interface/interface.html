⚠️ Common Fraud Signs

  • No company logo or vague company info
  • Promises high pay with no experience required
  • Asks for personal info upfront (SSN, bank details)
  • Requires payment for training or starter kit
  • Uses personal email (Gmail, Yahoo)
  • Guarantees job without interview
  • Vague or very short job description
  • Remote job with unrealistic earnings

🔍 How It Works

  • Structured fields (industry, education, logo) are one-hot encoded
  • Description + requirements are analyzed with TF-IDF (500 features)
  • Both are combined and passed to a Random Forest classifier
  • Threshold tuned to 0.35 for better fraud recall
  • class_weight=balanced handles the imbalanced dataset
